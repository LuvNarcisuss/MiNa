# MiNa artwork

The owner supplied the original boards and slice archives in the design directory. The product design links each board. The source-map file scripts/design-sources.json uses URL-encoded original archive names and English destination names. scripts/import-art.py selects the two-times exports in archive order, preserves transparency and encodes them as WebP for the bundled UI. Numeric filenames are one-based within a group. Asset ownership and third-party publication permissions are supplied by the project owner.

The app icon and launch art are also provided as native asset catalog resources. The sign-in background uses the supplied sleeping cat, night sky and chat mark with its old wordmark removed. MiNa is rendered as accessible interface text. Native status, time, people, balances and permissions use current device or service values.

Room covers and scenery in rooms-extra are crops from the supplied home, room creation, dream banner and room boards. The moon-seat, star-pillow and moonlit-sea recordings were synthesized for MiNa using sustained harmonies, bells and waves. They contain no third-party recordings. Offline soundscapes are identified separately from live rooms. The store uses the supplied twenty-item collection, including the Triple Six design; the level page uses the original color and grayscale badges.

The wallet uses the original two-times Wallet exports. Slice 1 supplies the full-page background, 2 the back control, 3 the large star coin, 4 the section title and 6 the complete package coin. The repeated package exports use the same illustration. Repeating the complete coin also preserves the bottom of the final row when scrolling. Offer quantities and storefront prices are live text. The promotion artwork is reserved for a configured offer.

The [Night Journal collection](../supabase/collections/night-journal/README.md) includes eight distinct photographic portraits, three real-world video excerpts and five narrated dream stories. These assets live in private cloud storage. The collection records the individual source pages, creators and narration model.

The Feed companion button uses a 112 × 112 pixel crop from the supplied 780 × 1688 Feed board at (628, 1372). The standalone Feed exports omit this button. Only the surrounding photo was masked away. Post Details uses the original Post background, navigation controls, like states, Discussion heading, emoji and send artwork.
