## App and cloud

- MiNa uses SwiftUI screens, a native navigation stack and bundled artwork on iPhones running iOS 16.0 or newer. The app, scheme and target are named MiNa. The bundle identifier is com.mina.bedtime. The native URLSession client uses the existing service. Device state and media retain their original owner directories. Release signing and App Store products require owner-provided identifiers.
- The iPhone client uses the Tokyo project owned by Thingtill Org, reference `weieitgfketnpvvwvfpa`. The Gmail-linked Tokyo project `nojpfyqctfwdshqnzudp` is the migration source. The owner explicitly approved permanent removal of Toli. The dashboard acknowledged deletion, and the organization listing subsequently contained MiNa and Sese.
- The entry screen uses Supabase email and password authentication. The existing private `auth.users.email` and `auth.users.encrypted_password` columns hold the email and salted password hash. The client can link an existing device identity to a verified email and retains its original UUID. Administrators can create confirmed email/password accounts in Authentication > Users. Cold launch requires a fresh sign-in. Password sessions are memory-only, and an unlinked device account retains its original Keychain item. On November 24, 2026, schema inspection confirmed both columns and the public Auth settings reported email registration enabled with email confirmation required. The email flow has not been exercised on a device.
- The deployed database covers profiles, visibility, follows, blocks, reports, posts, dreams, private media, comments, reactions, messages, calling, room presence, seats, audio signaling, gifts, wallet records and support. The economy deployment recorded here predates the wallet purchase migration. The app uses signed media URLs and user-scoped requests.
- Central plus opens AI Companion. The three illustrated topic cards work without a model. After the user agrees to share messages, the conversation requests Groq replies through the authenticated companion-chat function. The service provides renewable, free 60-minute sessions and a ten-minute warning. No third-party inference key is embedded.
- Profile feedback submits to a protected support table. The owner requested that the public support email stay blank. The in-app agreement text and publicly readable PDFs use the support form as the contact channel. Public GET responses for both PDFs matched the checked-in documents byte for byte.
- Room audio and one-to-one calls use WebRTC and authenticated, participant-scoped signaling. A foreground receiver can answer or decline. Production TURN configuration, background incoming-call notifications and physical-device audio acceptance remain outstanding.
- Twenty gifts and VIP0 through VIP12 are available in the interface. Gift delivery, inventory, spending and check-in use server-side transactions. The wallet now requests seven consumables from StoreKit 1. Checkout checks the server catalog and account before submitting a payment. The wallet purchase migration and receipt verification function are deployed. The seven remote package quantities and the service-only credit permission were checked. Apple sandbox and production checkout remain unverified. The application does not charge to create rooms, request music or start a companion session.
