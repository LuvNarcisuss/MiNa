# MiNa product design

MiNa is a bedtime voice community for listening to a live room, joining a quiet conversation, or leaving a short dream recording for others to hear later. The first useful action is listening. Starting a room, speaking, sharing a post and buying a gift are deliberate choices. This proposal covers the supplied iPhone designs and their supporting interactions.

## Design evidence and navigation

The design folder contains 60 PNG boards. The bundled board archive matches the loose files byte for byte. The 58 slice archives contain 1,683 PNGs in 561 groups of three export scales. Groups are counted within their source archive, so an icon used on several screens contributes several groups. The boards include screens, alternate states, composite overlays, an app icon, tab assets, gift art, level badges and a small standalone graphic.

| Surface | Proposed route | Evidence and interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rooms | First tab and default landing page | Rooms icon, “Voice Rooms” and “Followed”, search, ranking, dream banner, hot and recommended rooms. Floating microphone opens room creation. |
| Feed | Second tab | Highlighted feed icon, recommended and followed feeds, search and an activity bell. The floating chat button opens AI Companion. |
| Central plus | Companion landing | AI landing retains the central plus with all four outer icons inactive. The owner confirmed that the central plus opens AI Companion. Place “Create room”, “Post” and “Record dream” on the relevant surfaces, with a visible post action on the feed. |
| Messages | Fourth position | Highlighted message icon, conversation search, people strip, notifications and conversation rows. |
| Profile | Fifth position | Highlighted profile icon, social counts, edit, posts, dreams, wallet and settings. Use its search field for people as a proposed default. |

Keep the original teal and blue gradients, starry night rooms, moon and cloud artwork, sleeping cat, AI robot and purple star currency. Keep room controls readable over dark art and content readable on light mint surfaces. Give icon-only controls English accessibility names, a touch target of at least 44 points and a distinguishable state beyond color. Respect reduced motion for waves, rings and gift reactions. Keep text, counts, prices, permissions and timers live even where slices include baked-in text.

## Entry and account

Use the original starry sign-in illustration behind the centered MiNa wordmark. A gradual midnight-blue overlay supports translucent credential fields, mint policy links and the mint-to-sky primary button. Offer sign-in with a confirmed email and password provisioned in Supabase Authentication. A previous device account can link its email and set a password while keeping its user ID. Prompt for credentials on each cold launch and keep the renewable email session in memory. Signing out clears that session. Show authentication errors on the form. Make terms and privacy information available from entry and settings. Replace the old “Voice Night” branding wherever it is embedded in artwork.

## Live rooms

1. Discover a room through Hot Rooms, Recommended, Followed or search. Cards show cover, title, description, visibility, occupancy and elapsed time when available. “More” opens the matching full list. Keep search results separate from fallback recommendations. Recheck access when joining from any entry point.
2. A host selects a cover, enters a title of up to 20 characters, chooses a topic, sets visibility to Public, Followers Only or Only Me, and enables or disables following, gifts and comments in the room. Light music, bedtime stories and night chat are illustrated topics. Show a configured creation cost before confirmation if applicable. A failed creation must leave the user with the original balance.
3. Open into listen-only mode. Show the host seat, eight illustrated guest positions in two rows, available seats, speaker and mute states, participant count, elapsed time, chat and room controls. Request microphone access on an explicit speaking action. The room stage fits in one iPhone viewport. Its chat entry, microphone, gift and music controls stay above the bottom safe area. A reserved chat lane shows the newest message immediately and cycles through recent messages. Earlier messages retain their history label. The chat panel shows the full available conversation. The seat drawing does not establish a listener limit.
4. A listener taps an open seat or mic control, joins a queue when needed, sees position and can cancel. A proposed host control admits or declines a request. On admission, enable mute, output selection and leaving the mic while continuing to listen. The on-mic sheet also shows elapsed time, new followers and gifts for the session. Keep active seats, departures and queue changes synchronized.
5. The stage and audience sheet show connected listeners and earlier chat contributors with their profile names and portraits. The audience count and microphone seats reflect active membership only. Each chat message opens its author and shows its age. Messages from before the viewer or author joined the current session, including messages from departed authors, are marked as earlier conversation. The host card exposes room description, audience, duration, shares and gifts. The audience card shows profile, recent post, follow and gift actions. Share a room card through a selected friend's conversation and show an ended-room state for expired invitations.
6. The music drawer shows tracks, price if applicable, and play/pause states. Proposed default gives the host control of shared playback and listeners a request action. Use licensed recordings and show the actual playing state. The gift catalog opens even when sending is unavailable and explains the room or recipient restriction. The gift sheet selects a recipient, a free or priced gift and quantity. Show total before sending and deliver once.
7. Distinguish “Leave room” for a listener, “Leave mic” for a speaker and “End room” for the host. Ending closes the room for every participant, stops its audio, releases seats and clears the queue. On interruption, show reconnecting and reacquire the current role before enabling audio transmission. Avoid automatic unmuting.
8. Room and gift rankings support this week, last week, time remaining, podium positions, unfilled ranks, extended rows and the user's rank. Preserve check-in and like interactions. Proposed room score uses distinct check-in days in the selected period and gift score uses confirmed gift value. Exact scope, tie rules and rollover time need product approval. Check-in is repeat-safe within its configured day and scope.

For bedtime listening, the proposed default keeps an active room audible after screen lock and provides an obvious way to stop playback. A speaker should see the lock-screen microphone policy before joining the mic. Whether to keep transmitting or mute on lock is an open decision. A phone interruption suspends the session and requires deliberate mic reactivation.

## Dream Space

The home banner leads to a square of recorded dreams with author, text, time, waveform and duration. Follow an author, play or pause, like, save or open the report menu. Only one dream plays at once. Own entries appear in My Dreams with deletion.

The composer takes optional mood text and a required audible recording as a proposed rule. Record, stop, listen, re-record, save the take to the composer, then explicitly publish. Support recordings longer than zero and up to 60 seconds. Stop at the cap and retain the take for preview. Save and Publish have separate effects. Denied microphone access, an interruption or upload failure preserves recoverable work. A deleted dream drops out of the square and profile, and old references show unavailable content.

## Feed and activity

Recommended and Following lists show author, follow state, timestamp, caption, media, likes, comments and more actions. The composer specifically calls for video upload, while the cards show still cover frames. Propose video posts with a cover and a short mood or caption for the first release. Draft a photo-specific flow only if separately approved. Allow preview, media removal, upload progress and retry without duplicating a post.

Post Details opens with the caption and like control over the video, followed by the author, follow action and discussion. The emoji/text composer stays above the bottom safe area and keyboard. Post videos use the available content width in a 16:9 landscape viewport. Portrait footage fills it with a centered, proportional crop. My Posts supports owner deletion with confirmation. Comments and Likes are distinct notification views. Each groups the actor, time, source preview and a link back to the post. A removed original has a clear unavailable state.

## AI companion

The AI landing has Start Chat and three illustrated topic sheets. Sleep Tips offers four sleep habits. Emotion offers five supportive suggestions. Life Advice has emotional and everyday suggestions. Each sheet opens and closes independently. Proposed default makes these reference cards free to read.

Users agree to share their prompt and excerpts from up to six recent messages with Groq before sending. The server checks the signed-in account and active session before requesting a reply.

Start Chat shows the price and time allowance before activation. The conversation has distinct user and AI messages, timestamps, suggested prompts, emoji/text input, a help entry and remaining time. The reminder board depicts an allowance of 60 minutes and a warning below 10 minutes. Their production values and how the clock runs need approval. Display a low-time reminder with “Add time” and “Later”. Expiry preserves conversation history and offers explicit renewal. A failed activation must not consume credit. During an inference outage, retain the prompt and expose retry. Keep AI identifiable in every conversation. Replace “Guitar Assistant” with “MiNa Companion”.

## People, conversations and notifications

- Separate following, followers and friends. Proposed default defines friends as mutual follows. Update counts and actions on profile, cards and lists after the relationship change is saved.
- The public profile opens with an edge-to-edge portrait, name, bio and four counts for following, followers, mutual friends and visible video posts. The rounded gradient panel below contains Chat, Follow and the Historical Posts and Previous dreams tabs, with the latest matching work and a link to the full list. Editing covers nickname, photo, country, gender, birthday, language and a signature with the illustrated 50-word limit.
- Messages shows search, a people strip, system notifications, private conversations, timestamps and unread badges. An empty inbox shows an illustrated welcome card with a Find people action. A search with no matches shows a separate search result state. Swipe a conversation to the right to pin or unpin it, mark it read or unread, or delete it after confirmation. Pins and manual unread reminders are stored on this iPhone for the signed-in account. The most recently pinned conversation appears first. Opening a conversation clears its reminder. Deletion clears the signed-in user's copy through the selected message. Use the broom for “Mark all as read” as a proposed default. Clearing unread counts has its own operation.
- Mutual follows unlock the profile Chat action, private messaging and both on-device call previews. The composer and call controls are disabled for nonfriends, including in existing conversations. Unfollowing preserves history. Blocks and recipient privacy settings continue to govern direct messages and live calls. Previews open muted. Only tapping the microphone requests permission and starts local input metering. Muting or leaving the preview releases the recording session. Video also requests camera access and shows the front camera with a switch where supported. The speaker button selects speakerphone or automatic output. A silent preview retains that choice and applies it when audio starts. The speaker icon shows the selected routing mode. An unavailable camera uses a crossed-out video icon. Tapping a permission-denied camera or microphone opens Settings. Closing the preview releases both inputs. Previews do not contact the listener or create call history.
- Private chat supports text, emoji, failure retry and one-to-one voice calling. Include incoming, ringing, accepted, declined, busy, missed, disconnected and ended states. Start the call clock on connection. Provide mute, speaker output and hang-up. Require a choice before switching from a live microphone session to a call.
- Route platform, follow and ranking notices through System Notifications. Open the applicable announcement, profile or ranking from its category. Synchronize unread counts across badges, lists and detail views.

## Wallet, store and levels

The seven consumable packages use the owner-supplied US price schedule. The Wallet board supplies the single-column offers, full-color purple star coins, balance illustration and pastel background. The configured catalog has no timed promotion.

Use a single star-coin balance. Show real coin packages and localized StoreKit prices. Render a promotion only for a configured offer with an actual end time. Purchases grant coins once after verified transactions. Pending, canceled and failed purchases leave the balance unchanged. Keep a ledger for credits, debits and reversals.

Use one spending confirmation for room creation, music requests, gifts, store items and AI time when priced. Show the item or action, recipient if applicable, quantity, total and balance. On insufficient funds, open the wallet and restore the user's selection on return. Require explicit confirmation before retrying a spend.

Store and Backpack are tabs of a single feature. The store layouts include 20 themed item designs, while the separate gift art board contains 19 assets. The “666” item is present in the store slices. A proposed inventory model shows owned quantities and allows one inventory unit to be sent as a gift without charging its price again. Grant or consume inventory atomically with the associated transaction.

The level screen shows accumulated progress, the next threshold and VIP0 through VIP12. Each level has colored and gray badge art. Show the earned level beside the user in rooms and profiles. Treat VIP as progression. The board offers no subscription checkout. The eligible actions, thresholds, coins, limits and offer catalog remain configurable. The numbers printed on the boards are design examples.

## Privacy, moderation and account controls

The board named for privacy is a settings page with profile privacy, online status, messaging and voice-call permissions, plus User Agreement and Privacy Policy links. Define a consistent interaction policy across discovery, search, room invitations, chat and calls. Proposed default always gives a block precedence over following or friendship. Explain which fields remain public when profile privacy is enabled.

Show Report and Block on content and user menus. Live rooms expose room and host reporting from the stage, plus reporting and confirmed blocking on other participants’ cards. Report collects the target, reason and optional context and returns a real receipt after submission. Block stops new direct contact and invitations. The blacklist offers explicit unblock. Proposed room moderation lets the host mute or remove disruptive speakers. Reports need a review destination and handling process. Profile also provides Contact Us, Community Guidelines, sign out and account deletion. Account deletion requires a separate confirmation, account identity verification and a defined service-side data disposition.

## Copy and interaction corrections

Use “My Dreams” on the owner's dream list. Replace the room-exit sheet's “Quick barrage” and “Unlock now” with role-specific actions. Replace “Video duration” on the mic sheet with “Time on mic”. Replace nail-art and climbing profile copy with context-appropriate English for authored content. Preserve actual user bios. Use “Post”, “Following”, “Likes” and “Sign out” consistently. Show only one current state where boards overlay selected and unselected controls, empty and populated notifications, or balance and spending confirmations.

## Release acceptance

| Journey | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Enter and listen | A user can find a live room, join muted, hear a real speaker, leave and stop receiving audio. |
| Speak and moderate | Canceling frees a queue position. A connected speaker can mute and leave the seat. Host closure ends the room on all clients. |
| Record and publish | A 60-second take stops at the limit. Preview does not publish. A retry creates one dream that plays from the square and can be deleted by its owner. |
| Social and safety | Comments open their source. Private settings and blocks are enforced for calls and messages. A removed source opens an unavailable state. |
| Paid activity | Each verified purchase credits once. A duplicate gift request consumes once. A canceled confirmation or failed activation leaves the correct balance. |
| AI | Successful activation starts the configured entitlement. The warning and expiry refer to the same remaining time. Failed inference has a visible retry state. |

Confirm the intended age audience and eligibility for direct contact, private calls, purchases and gifting before finalizing account entry and the associated permissions. The birthday field alone does not establish an age policy.

Product decisions before implementation include account recovery, paid actions and catalog, AI metering, gift inventory rules and ranking formulas. Service dependencies include authentication, realtime audio and presence, messaging, media storage, moderation, AI inference, StoreKit products and verified transaction processing. Music rights and publication rights for reference photography require confirmation before using those media in the app.
